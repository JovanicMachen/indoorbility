# Two-minute Indoorbility demonstration

## Before presenting

Build onto the phone and test permissions. If demonstrating live indoor guidance, first complete a real reviewed hallway walk and validate its accuracy. Do not present simulator movement or fictional data as real localization. Network discovery requires connectivity; the indoor/demo engines work locally.

## 0:00–0:25 — Destination-first navigation

Open Navigate. Explain: “You choose a room; the app remembers the measured path, rather than making you manage scan folders.” Show Remember a new route. On LiDAR hardware, briefly show the real wireframe preview. Otherwise state that the simulator cannot capture a space.

## 0:25–1:05 — Campus routing demonstration

Open Explore the demo building, choose Room 204, confirm Entrance, and show Directions via Lift A. This is the fictional Learning Center fixture. Report Lift A closed and confirm: the route changes to Lift B. Report Lift B too: no verified step-free alternative is invented. Show report resolution.

If a real hallway was mapped and field-tested beforehand, show its room entry separately: Go with camera, entrance/map recognition or explicit fallback, automatic progress, and room arrival confirmation. Do not imply the fictional campus coordinates match the room you are standing in.

## 1:05–1:40 — Shopping assistant

Assistant → enter “Buy milk, cereal, shampoo and paper towels” → Find a plan. Explain that the local fallback uses a fictional store catalog and an exact optimized multi-stop route. Mark an item collected, add an unknown product, and toggle the simulated dairy closure. Known stops recalculate; the unknown item is explicitly unlocated. Checkout remains last.

## 1:40–2:00 — Connected discovery and limits

Open Discover buildings with Google and search a real building. Google results are attributed; outdoor directions use a separate Apple search of your original query. Explain that GPS prepares the camera near a confirmed entrance but cannot assert indoor position. Cloud AI, live retail inventory and automatic unseen-building mapping are not claimed.
